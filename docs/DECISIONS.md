# Architecture and business decisions

## Stage 4 financial rules (supersede earlier deferred billing notes)

- INR amounts are nonnegative integer paise bounded by SQLite's signed 64-bit maximum for each persisted line and invoice. Rates are integer basis points (10,000 = 100%). Intermediate products and report batch sums use Python integers. Overflow rejects the entire subscription transaction; no saturation or floating point is used.
- Invoice periods are `[start, end)`. Daily/weekly boundaries add 1/7 days from the original start; monthly/yearly boundaries are calculated from the original start's day/month and clamped to the target month's last day. A later month/year recovers the original anchor, including February 29. Dates outside Python's calendar range fail explicitly.
- A full period is due when its start is <= explicit `as_of` and <= inclusive subscription expiry. No proration: the last full period may extend beyond expiry. Invoice date is period start; due date adds accepted payment terms in calendar days. No wall-clock lookup determines eligibility.
- Currently paused subscriptions generate nothing and retain their next date. Resume catches up every deferred period; there are no historical pause intervals and pausing never forgives charges. Active accepted `auto_close=true` subscriptions close only after expiry, after all eligible periods commit. This applies independently of manual `closable`. Paused subscriptions never auto-close. With auto_close=false the next scheduled date stays beyond expiry for a possible renewal.
- Plan base is a separate quantity-one line with zero tax: plans have no tax field. Product line prices, quantities, descriptions and exclusive tax rates come from accepted subscription snapshots, never current catalog prices. Invoice lines and calculated header values are immutable, including draft invoices. Only status may transition; all invoices permanently reserve their unique subscription/period, including cancelled invoices.
- Discounts use inclusive validity dates evaluated at period start. Minimum purchase uses the whole pre-discount invoice subtotal. Minimum quantity counts all product quantities for subscription scope, or matching product quantities for product scope; plan quantity is excluded. Fixed rules require a value only; percentage rules require a rate only. Product scope requires a product; subscription scope forbids one. Global usage limits are supported; per-customer limits are deferred because Stage 4 has no such field.
- At most one active eligible discount applies. Compute its exact nominal discount (percentage half-up `(base * bps + 5000) // 10000`, fixed capped at eligible subtotal). Allocate it proportionally using integer floors, then distribute remaining paise by descending fractional remainder, with original line order as tie-breaker (plan first, products by subscription-line ID). Allocations sum exactly and never exceed subtotals. Each exclusive line tax is half-up on that line's discounted taxable amount. Select the greatest actual grand-total saving (including tax reduction), tie-breaking by lowest discount ID. Zero-saving rules consume no usage. Totals are subtotal minus allocations plus line taxes.
- Invoice numbers are stored generated `INV-%06d` values from SQLite AUTOINCREMENT IDs. Redemption, counter increment, invoice/header/lines, schedule advance and audits share one BEGIN IMMEDIATE transaction per subscription, rechecking identity and eligibility after locking. Failures roll back only that subscription; processing continues in ID order. Period uniqueness is the final duplicate safeguard. Existing reserved periods are reported separately and never redeemed again.
- Preview uses a single read snapshot and the same period/pricing functions. It simulates global usage in subscription-ID/period order, restores simulated consumption on a failed subscription, and writes no invoices, audits, schedules, counters or redemptions. Actual execution independently rechecks each subscription; concurrent edits can change the outcome relative to an earlier preview.
- Draft invoices may confirm. Draft/confirmed invoices may cancel. Paid is reserved for Stage 5; paid/cancelled transitions are rejected. Cancellation retains period reservation and historical redemptions and does not restore discount usage. No payment history, customer balance, refunds, PDFs, email or reports are implemented here.
- Billing reports distinguish proposed/generated invoices, already-reserved periods, skipped subscriptions (including paused or concurrently processed), failures, and projected automatic closures. Counts describe these explicit arrays; report totals sum only new proposals/generated invoices. A repeat run with no due work is a successful zero-invoice run, not a new financial transaction.

## Previously accepted foundation rules

1. **INR only.** No exchange rates or multi-currency accounting.
2. **Integer paise.** Persist and transport monetary amounts as integers; never use binary floating point for financial calculations.
3. **Integer basis points.** Represent percentage and tax rates with 10,000 basis points equal to 100%.
4. **Half-up rounding.** Round only at the final calculation step for each applicable amount. For a nonnegative paise base `p` and rate `b`, the final percentage amount is `(p * b + 5000) // 10000`. Keep intermediate values exact using integer/rational arithmetic or Decimal. Define applicable line/document amounts and discount/tax order before billing implementation; do not repeatedly round intermediate steps.
5. **Half-open invoice periods.** `[start, end)` includes the start and excludes the end, allowing adjacent periods without double billing.
6. **Preserve monthly billing anchor.** A subscription anchored on the 31st bills on February's last day, then March 31st. Compute from the original anchor, not from the previous clamped date.
7. **No proration.** The hackathon MVP does not prorate mid-period commercial changes.
8. **Immutable historical calculations.** Issued invoices retain their original prices, rates, and calculated amounts after catalog edits.
9. **Snapshot on confirmation.** Subscriptions retain relevant agreed commercial configuration when confirmed.
10. **Plan plus additional lines.** Plan price is a recurring base charge; subscription product lines add to that charge.
11. **Offline runtime.** Application execution requires no internet. Dependency installation and future optional SMTP delivery are separate concerns; a local demo must work without SMTP.
12. **Deferred frontend.** Use Next.js, TypeScript, and Tailwind, integrating exported Google Stitch designs in a later stage. No frontend in Stage 1.

## Foundation implementation

- This Git repository's root (currently named `Odoo`) is the `subcadence/` project root in the requested layout; avoid an unnecessary nested repository. Work on `main` and never push automatically. The Stage 4 brief requests verified commits at meaningful milestones.
- Relative SQLite URLs resolve against `backend/`, not the current working directory. Default: `backend/data/subcadence.db`. Create parent directories only when constructing an engine; importing the app neither connects nor migrates.
- Every new physical connection enables foreign keys and a 5,000 ms busy timeout. File databases must enable WAL or connection establishment fails. Memory databases skip WAL and use a shared StaticPool for local/test use; production development uses a file database.
- Disable sqlite3 legacy implicit transaction handling. SQLAlchemy emits explicit `BEGIN DEFERRED`; a caller can set `sqlite_begin_mode="IMMEDIATE"` on a connection before its transaction. Do not also configure driver autocommit/isolation options. This follows [SQLAlchemy's SQLite transaction guidance](https://docs.sqlalchemy.org/en/20/dialects/sqlite.html#enabling-non-legacy-sqlite-transactional-modes-with-the-sqlite3-or-aiosqlite-driver).
- A request owns its session. Business services will own `with session.begin():` write boundaries. Cleanup rolls back unfinished transactions; dependencies never auto-commit.
- Alembic is the only normal schema migration mechanism. Model imports register metadata centrally. SQLite migrations use batch rendering for future alterations; review autogenerated migrations before applying them.
- `users.email` retains SQLite `NOCASE` and UNIQUE. Stage 2 accepts ASCII emails only, validates syntax without DNS, trims surrounding whitespace, and stores lowercase canonical addresses. Internationalized addresses are rejected instead of claiming Unicode uniqueness. Role, active-state, and nonnegative auth-version CHECK constraints protect direct SQL writes.
- SQLite AUTOINCREMENT prevents reuse of deleted numeric IDs. Audit actor foreign keys use `ON DELETE SET NULL` to retain historical events. No user-delete endpoint exists.
- Persist timestamps as naive UTC SQLite DATETIME values; typed ORM reads return timezone-aware UTC. ORM timestamp writes reject naive values. SQL defaults use `CURRENT_TIMESTAMP`; `updated_at` changes on ORM updates. Future direct-SQL writers must explicitly update `updated_at`; there is no trigger.
- Stage 2 uses Argon2id via argon2-cffi. One validator requires 9–128 characters, uppercase, lowercase, and a special character; no digit required and no password modification. No fallback JWT secret is provided.
- Structured audit details use JSON; replace the value to persist changes (in-place nested mutation tracking is not enabled).
- Settings load the project-root `.env`, with real environment variables taking precedence, following [pydantic-settings configuration](https://docs.pydantic.dev/latest/concepts/pydantic_settings/). CORS accepts explicit HTTP(S) origins only, with credentials; local default is `http://localhost:3000`.
- Swagger UI v5.34.1 assets, licenses, provenance, and checksums are vendored under `backend/app/static/swagger-ui/`. `/docs` serves them locally with the external schema validator disabled, following [FastAPI's self-hosted documentation guidance](https://fastapi.tiangolo.com/how-to/custom-docs-ui-assets/). No documentation CDN or business frontend is required.

## Stage 2 authentication and reset decisions

- HS256 only; access tokens expire in 7,200 seconds and require `sub`, `iat`, `exp`, `auth_version`, `iss=subcadence-api`, and `aud=subcadence`. Validate signatures/registered claims with PyJWT, and strictly validate integer times, a positive SQLite-range subject, a nonnegative integer version, and the two-hour lifetime using the injected UTC clock. Never use JWT role claims for authority. See [PyJWT claim verification](https://pyjwt.readthedocs.io/en/latest/api.html).
- Signing keys require at least 32 UTF-8 bytes and 8 distinct characters with no surrounding whitespace. These checks reject obvious weak values; they do not measure entropy. Generate keys using `secrets.token_urlsafe(48)`. Missing/insecure configuration returns a generic 503 for auth operations while health/docs remain available.
- Immutable principals contain current database identity, role, active state, creation time, and auth version. Authentication uses and closes its own read-only-by-convention session, never touching the request business session. No silent commit/rollback of business work. Sensitive write services recheck principal validity/role after acquiring their write lock.
- Explicit logout and successful reset increment `users.auth_version`, invalidating all previously issued tokens. Existing users receive zero during the new migration. There are no refresh tokens or per-device sessions. Concurrent requests already authorized before revocation cannot be recalled; sensitive writes recheck version transactionally.
- Reset tokens use `secrets.token_urlsafe(32)` (256 random bits), SHA-256 digests, 30-minute UTC expiry, and single use. Issuance consumes previously unused records and commits with the offline email. Redemption locks with `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` before reading token state, then commits the password, consumption, auth version, and audit together. Expiry is checked after obtaining the lock. Concurrent consumption tests use distinct physical file-backed SQLite connections.
- The mailer is injectable. No SMTP settings selects DatabaseOutboxMailer. Any supplied SMTP field selects SMTPMailer; incomplete configuration fails delivery without silently switching to a local outbox. STARTTLS uses the default certificate-verifying SSL context, is enabled by default, and is mandatory with credentials. Unauthenticated non-STARTTLS SMTP is available only for deliberate local relay setups. Delivery timeout defaults to 5 seconds.
- SMTP delivery occurs inside reset issuance's transaction for this MVP. Failure rolls back the replacement and preserves prior valid links, while returning the generic success response. External delivery cannot be rolled back if SMTP accepts the message and the later DB commit fails; such a link will not be usable. There is no retry worker or production delivery guarantee. Outbox bodies intentionally contain raw reset links; only the authoritative reset-token table is hash-only.
- `APP_ENV=development` registers the admin-only paginated outbox endpoint. `test` and `production` do not register it, including in OpenAPI. Protect/clear sensitive local databases as appropriate; no outbox retention worker is implemented. `PASSWORD_RESET_BASE_URL` points to the future frontend, not a reset page implemented here.
- Validation error responses omit submitted inputs/context, and credential/outbox responses use `Cache-Control: no-store`. Audit details contain no credentials, hashes, JWTs, or raw reset tokens. Account creation, staff creation, reset, logout, and initial admin creation are audited in the mutation transaction.
- Explicit admin seeding validates environment inputs, preserves existing admins unchanged, and refuses promotion of a non-admin account. It never runs on application startup. The seed command needs a migrated DB but does not need a signing key to hash/create an admin; API auth still requires the key.
- This is a hackathon implementation, not production-hardened authentication: no rate limiting, refresh rotation, MFA, delivery retries, or security monitoring. Generic public messages reduce account disclosure but equal request timing is not guaranteed. Future frontend clients should keep access tokens in memory, send Bearer headers, reauthenticate after expiry, and clear tokens after logout; no frontend or cookie auth is implemented.

## Stage 3 commercial decisions

- CustomerProfile is one-to-one with a customer-role User. Signup and staff/admin account creation insert the profile transactionally. Existing customer accounts are backfilled by migration, retaining credentials and audits. Minimal legacy/signup profiles start with empty company name and the email local part as contact name; users can enrich them later. Signup also accepts optional company_name/contact_name/phone. Staff-created customers require nonempty company/contact names. User/account IDs are never writable through profile PATCH. Notes are visible on the owning profile but writable only by operators.
- Product types are `goods` and `service`. Each line selects one optional attribute/value variant, whose extra price adds to sales price. Product/variant pairs use composite foreign keys referencing an explicitly unique `(id, product_id)` pair, following [SQLAlchemy composite-constraint guidance](https://docs.sqlalchemy.org/en/20/core/constraints.html). Variant attribute/value must be unique per product.
- All price/amount inputs are strict JSON integers in 0–9,223,372,036,854,775,807. SQLite checks integer storage and nonnegative bounds. Reject overflow in unit-price addition or quantity multiplication before binding. No floats are used for commercial calculations. Quantities are 1–1,000,000; a new subscription/template supports at most 100 lines. Payment terms are 0–3650 days, quotation validity 1–3650 days. These MVP bounds are explicit abuse/overflow limits.
- A product has at most one optional exclusive tax. Rates are 0–10,000 basis points (0–100%). Stage 3 stores/snapshots the rate and performs no tax-total/invoice calculation. Tax treatment of the recurring plan base charge and combined discounts remains a later billing decision.
- Plan dates are inclusive availability for new sales. They do not truncate subscriptions. New Draft/Quotation creation, Draft-to-Quotation, and confirmation require an active plan available on explicit as_of, active products/variants/taxes, and total product quantity meeting min_quantity. Plan price is a recurring base charge; product lines are additional charges, not a replacement for the base price.
- Flags are independent. Defaults: auto_close=false, closable=true, pausable=false, renewable=true, active=true. auto_close is stored only; no scheduler is introduced. After confirmation, accepted flags are used for closure/pause/renewal rather than mutable plan settings.
- Every subscription starts Draft and has a stored generated number `printf('SUB-%06d', id)` based on its database AUTOINCREMENT key. Numbers are unique, concurrency-safe, and expand beyond six digits without truncation. There is no count-plus-one allocation.
- New Draft lines/plan base show indicative prices. Confirmation recalculates from then-current catalog values and freezes line unit prices, amounts, rates, labels, plan base, billing period, flags, and terms. Catalog changes after quotation but before acceptance may therefore affect final accepted prices; no earlier price-lock guarantee is claimed. There is no subscription edit API in Stage 3.
- Manual quotations default to 30 validity days; template quotations use template validity_days. Quotation expiry is as_of + validity_days, inclusive for confirmation. Confirmation cannot predate the quotation or exceed subscription expiry. No implicit current-date lookup occurs in domain logic. The injected UTC clock is used only for audit/record timestamps, independently of explicit business dates.
- Template creation may start with zero lines for administration, but subscription creation rejects insufficient total quantity. Template edits replace their own lines; copied subscriptions retain independent line rows and expiry. Template-line CRUD is scoped to its parent and paginated.
- Activation requires as_of within subscription dates, retains start_date as the original billing anchor, and sets next_invoice_date=start_date even after a delay. No invoice is generated. Accepted subscriptions can activate after their plan's sales availability ends or catalog items are deactivated.
- Only admin/staff can create subscriptions, quote, confirm, activate, close, pause/resume, or renew. Customers can read only their own profiles/subscriptions and edit basic own-profile fields. SQL ownership predicates precede counting, pagination, filters, and detail lookup; another customer's IDs return 404. Admin configures all catalogs/templates; staff has read-only catalog access and operational customer/subscription access.
- Pause is a boolean on Active, not a lifecycle status. Repeat pause/unpaused resume are conflicts. Pause/resume never alter next_invoice_date or the start anchor. Renewal is Active-only (including paused or expired-but-not-Closed), requires a later expiry than old expiry/as_of, and changes no prices or billing date. Initial expiry remains in the immutable snapshot, with renewed dates recorded in audits. Closure is Active-only with accepted closable flag, clears pause and next_invoice_date, and is terminal.
- SQLite CHECK/FK constraints and migration-owned triggers protect role linkage, nonnegative/integer values, valid dates/enum/boolean states, variant ownership, minimum quantity on quotation/confirmation, normal transitions, and frozen accepted terms/lines. Normal API writes use BEGIN IMMEDIATE with current-role rechecks and mutation/audit in the same transaction. Authentication still uses its independent read session. Migrations 0001/0002 remain unchanged.
- No hard-delete endpoints exist for catalog, plan, customer, or subscription records. Historical references use RESTRICT. Template lines may be deleted because copied quotations no longer reference them. No billing, invoice, discount, payment, report, frontend, automatic expiry, or proration implementation is part of Stage 3.

## Stitch frontend integration

- The local frontend uses Next.js App Router, TypeScript, and Tailwind. The five Stitch exports and DESIGN.md are visual references, not executable application pages. Palette, typography, shell, cards, lifecycle, billing console, and invoice sections are retained; no iframes or Tailwind CDN are used.
- Fonts are bundled through Fontsource and icons through Lucide. An inline SVG fixes the broken brand image; initials replace the fictional profile image. Local font/icon license files are included. Navigation is limited to implemented frontend screens and respects server-returned roles.
- Authentication uses the existing JSON login/me/logout contracts and an in-memory API client/React context. No localStorage, sessionStorage, cookie, URL, or token logging persistence. Refresh/new tab requires sign-in. Logout is reported only after server acknowledgement; protected 401s clear the local session. Backend authentication and authorization are unchanged.
- Monetary response tokens are decoded losslessly into BigInt paise, including SQLite-range values. Formatting and base-plus-line addition use integer arithmetic. Displayed subscription charges are explicitly before tax; no invoice totals, tax allocations, MRR, ARR, collections, or trends are fabricated. Non-money identifiers/counts outside JavaScript's safe-integer range fail explicitly.
- Customer/plan/product/variant selectors page/search against actual APIs instead of loading entire tables. Subscription lists use real server limit/offset, status/plan filters, and number search. Customer-role readers never query administrative catalog/plan configuration. Accepted snapshots take priority over current catalog descriptions/flags.
- Subscription actions use explicit business-date dialogs and exact existing schemas. There is no subscription price/line edit UI, quarterly frequency, proration, automatic-renewal claim, or invoice-generation shortcut. The backend decides validity and success; the UI never announces success early.
- Billing/invoice/payment screens are visibly unavailable and execution actions are disabled. Their layouts make no requests to invented endpoints. Dry-run preview, actual execution, already-billed/catch-up periods, failures, and totals are distinct future integration points. Prototype Odoo/gateway/cloud/webhook/worker/dunning claims and 2024 fictional records are removed.
- Browser tests use real FastAPI plus a migrated temporary SQLite file and synthetic data; they never use normal app data. Desktop/mobile screenshots support visual comparison. The frontend remains local-only; no hosted deployment is configured. Detailed setup and limitations are in frontend/README.md.
